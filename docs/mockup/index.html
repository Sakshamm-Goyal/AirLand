LandConnect

A clickable prototype of the land marketplace. Browse plots, list land with a map pin, chat about a listing and call the other person in the app. Nobody sees a phone number. The data is mock data, and calls here are simulated; the real app runs them over LiveKit.

View as

Role-based access: Buyers can't list land. Sellers and Brokers get the List land button, deal rooms and My listings.

Ring this phone

Accept, decline, reply with a message, or let it ring for 30 seconds to see a missed call. Ring it again during a call to see busy handling.
